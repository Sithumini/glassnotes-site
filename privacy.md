# GlassNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

GlassNotes is designed as a private, local-first macOS notes app.

## Information GlassNotes collects

GlassNotes does not require an account and does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics or profiling.

GlassNotes does not operate a backend service for storing your notes.

## Your notes

Your note content is stored in the folder you choose on your Mac.

GlassNotes does not upload your note content to GlassNotes-operated servers.

If you choose a folder that is synchronised by a third-party service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive, that service may process or synchronise those files according to its own privacy policy.

## Purchases

GlassNotes Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

GlassNotes uses Apple StoreKit to determine whether you have an active Annual or Lifetime entitlement.

GlassNotes does not receive or store your payment card details.

## Analytics and advertising

GlassNotes does not include third-party advertising.

GlassNotes does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Network services

GlassNotes does not require an account or GlassNotes-operated cloud service to use the app.

## Data sharing

GlassNotes does not sell personal information.

GlassNotes does not share note content with advertisers or data brokers.

## Data retention

GlassNotes does not retain copies of your note content on GlassNotes-operated servers.

Your notes remain in the folder you selected until you move or delete them.

## Children

GlassNotes is a general productivity app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if GlassNotes features or data practices change.
