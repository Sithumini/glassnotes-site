# GlassNotes Support

GlassNotes is a lightweight, local-first notes app for macOS.

## Getting help

If you need help with GlassNotes, please contact:

sithumini.senevirathne@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, it helps to include:

- your macOS version
- your GlassNotes version
- a short description of what happened
- steps to reproduce the issue, if possible

Please do not send private note content unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

## Where are my notes stored?

GlassNotes stores your notes in the folder you selected during setup.

Your notes are stored locally on your Mac unless you choose a folder that is synchronised by a third-party service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive.

GlassNotes does not require an account.

## Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased GlassNotes Pro:

1. Open GlassNotes.
2. Open Settings.
3. Select **Restore Purchases**.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.

## GlassNotes Pro

GlassNotes Free supports up to:

- 2 notes
- 2 groups

GlassNotes Pro supports up to:

- 100 notes
- 8 groups

Pro is available through:

- an annual subscription
- a one-time Lifetime purchase

If Pro access ends, your existing notes are not deleted. Notes above the Free limit remain visible and readable, and upgrading again restores full editing access.

## Troubleshooting

If GlassNotes cannot access your selected notes folder, open the app and choose an available folder again.

If a purchase does not appear immediately, try **Restore Purchases** from Settings.

For persistent issues, contact the support email above.
